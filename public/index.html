How it works

From payout list to live listing in about five minutes.

  1. Pick a market

    Choose a service and city from the list of top-paying Lead Smart markets, or let AI market picks choose for you.

  2. Add your number

    Paste your tracking number and brand. Pick an angle: same-day, seasonal, local, or a mix.

  3. Generate the kit

    Get unique titles, descriptions, an AI photo creative and Messenger replies for each listing.

  4. Post, test, scale

    Post across services and cities, see which ones bring qualified calls, and add more of those.

What's in the kit

Everything a listing needs, nothing that gets it pulled.

Listing writer

Unique titles and descriptions per city, so your posts don't read as duplicates.

AI photo creatives

Listing rules built in

The rules every listing follows.

✓Price field uses the $1,111 placeholder.
✕No real service prices in the listing.
✕No price quotes in Messenger. Replies point to a call.
✕Never says or implies the service costs nothing.
✕No invented reviews, licences or guarantees.
✓Unique wording per listing to avoid duplicate flags.
