How it works

From brief to reviewed draft in a few minutes.

  1. Write a brief

    Choose a service and a city. Optional AI market picks suggest where to post next from a reference list.

  2. Add your number

    Paste the tracking number from your network and pick a listing style: same-day, seasonal, local, or a mix.

  3. Generate drafts

    Get titles, descriptions, reply templates and optional AI images, each run through the network-rule checks.

  4. Review, edit, publish

    Read every draft, fix anything that isn't right, and confirm your channel permits the offer before you publish.

Network-rule checks

What the checks look for.

Checks drafts against the network rules configured in CallPosts. Passing these checks does not mean a platform permits your offer or guarantees approval.

✕No service prices, rates, discounts or coupons in drafts or replies.
✕No offers that say or imply the service costs nothing.
✕No invented reviews, ratings, licences, guarantees or warranties.
✕No links or web addresses in listing drafts.
✓States that calls connect to independent local providers.
✓Your tracking number appears in the draft.

Rule source: Lead Smart's publisher guidance for home-service offers, as supplied to CallPosts. Last reviewed October 21, 2026. Before you promote an offer on any channel, confirm that the channel permits it.
